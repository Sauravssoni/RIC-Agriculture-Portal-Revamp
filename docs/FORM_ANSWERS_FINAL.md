## Challenge — description of technology, problem addressed and proposed solution
RajKisan One upgrades the Agriculture Department’s public web front door without replacing award-winning RajKisan Saathi. A mobile-first Hindi/English PWA adds intent-based discovery, explainable rule-driven eligibility, SSO/Jan Aadhaar patterns, verified-document reuse/upload, application timelines, grounded Krishi Saathi, GIS access, feedback and first-party journey analytics over Rajasthan’s existing DPI.

## Value proposition
Syntheon offers a deployable Digital Front Door, not another parallel agriculture platform. Farmers get one personalised Action Centre for schemes, pending actions, documents, status, advisories and support. Officials get completion funnels, friction/search analytics, feedback, content/GIGW health and integration monitoring. Versioned rules, consent receipts and explicit authority states make every recommendation auditable.

## How the technology addresses the challenge
The 90-day pilot wraps WebMyWay content and RajKisan services with a responsive PWA, structured CMS migration and Raj Sewa Dwaar adapters. RajSSO/Jan Aadhaar support identity; Raj eVault/DigiLocker reduce repeat uploads; deterministic rules pre-check schemes; BHASHINI supports voice/language; RajDharaa supports GIS. Analytics converts citizen friction into owner/SLA actions. AI explains; Government systems decide.

## Available resource / USP
Most revamps stop at responsive UI, chatbot and page-view analytics. RajKisan One preserves Rajasthan’s proven RajKisan backbone and adds cross-channel continuity, Action Centre, human-approved machine-readable scheme rules, secure document readiness, service-quality observability, GIGW lifecycle governance and truthful DPI integration states. SUTRA-ID Edge selectively extends the same contracts to low-connectivity assisted field access.
