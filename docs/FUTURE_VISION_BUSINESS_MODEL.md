# RajKisan One — Future Vision, Joint Pilot & Sustainable Service Model

## North Star
Turn the Agriculture Department’s public digital touchpoint into a reusable **Agriculture Experience Layer**: the same governed service contract can power web/PWA, e-Mitra, extension camps, authorised field devices and future AgriStack/UFSI-aligned channels while authoritative data and decisions stay with Government systems.

## 90-day joint production-core pilot
**Partners proposed:** Department of Agriculture + DoIT&C/RISL + selected pilot districts + Syntheon Tech.

### Government provides
- nominated product owner, WIM/content owners and scheme-rule owners;
- approved pilot service set and official scheme sources;
- sandbox/credential onboarding for agreed integrations;
- pilot offices/field stakeholders and UAT/security coordination.

### Syntheon provides
- citizen front end/PWA, structured CMS layer and service catalogue;
- deterministic scheme compiler + Citizen Service Contracts;
- Krishi Saathi language/tool orchestration;
- analytics/service-observability command centre;
- approved integration adapters, security controls, documentation and training;
- selective SUTRA assisted-channel pilot where justified.

### Joint acceptance
Baseline → intervention → measured outcome. No statewide impact claim before pilot evidence.

## Expansion roadmap
### Phase A — Digital Front Door (0–90 days)
Priority services, GIGW/WCAG, SSO/identity pattern, document readiness, status, feedback, command centre.

### Phase B — Agriculture Experience Network (3–12 months)
Expand service contracts; e-Mitra/extension continuity; proactive deadlines; district content operations; authoritative GIS; reusable notification and grievance context.

### Phase C — Farmer Service Graph (12–24 months)
With authorised AgriStack/UFSI access, compose minimum-necessary farmer/parcel/service claims to pre-fill journeys and route citizens without creating a duplicate master database.

### Phase D — Rajasthan Agriculture Experience API (18–30 months)
Department-governed service contracts exposed to approved public channels and ecosystem partners; conformance tests prevent divergent eligibility/content experiences.

### Phase E — Predictive Service Delivery (24–36 months)
Use privacy-safe aggregate signals to identify service exclusion, seasonal demand, content gaps and assisted-service needs. AI may prioritise outreach; it cannot create entitlement facts.

## Optional capability modules
- **SUTRA Assisted Service Kit:** voice/evidence capture in low-connectivity camps with human confirmation and resumable service handoff.
- **FarmGraph Advisory Card:** governed crop/weather/field-risk context inside My Agriculture; never a statutory eligibility decision-maker.
- **Service Quality Lab:** controlled A/B testing of navigation/content with accessibility and completion guardrails.
- **Scheme Compiler Studio:** versioned policy-to-rule workflow with legal/departmental review and rollback.

## Sustainable Government service model
The commercial model should minimise lock-in and avoid per-farmer rent extraction.

1. **Implementation / production-core pilot fee** — scoped deliverables and acceptance gates.
2. **Annual managed application support** — SLA, security patches, accessibility monitoring, content tooling, connector observability and releases.
3. **Optional integration work orders** — new Department/DoIT&C-approved services or channels.
4. **Optional field/edge managed service** — device provisioning, support and operator workflows only where the Department elects to deploy SUTRA.
5. **Knowledge transfer + exit portability** — source, schemas, content/service contracts and runbooks are structured to remain Government-operable.

## Procurement-friendly principles
- open standards and API contracts;
- Government-owned citizen data and official content;
- no model lock-in for language/AI providers;
- no hidden eligibility model;
- auditable releases and rollback;
- measurable SLA/experience outcomes;
- phase-gated spend based on accepted capability.
